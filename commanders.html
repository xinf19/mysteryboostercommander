Tolabow, Loch Rascal

Tolabow interacts with instants and sorceries, and the set article describes its Rulebreaker permission as allowing the inclusion of red spells. [Source]

That suggests an instant-and-sorcery deck direction; when building around it, consult the card text update noted in the same article rather than relying on the printed wording alone. [Source]

The Unluckiest Planeswalker

The Unluckiest Planeswalker belongs to the Rulebreaker cycle described as allowing certain cards outside the commander's color identity and any basic land cards. [Source]

The cardlist identifies negative Auras and Curses as its best fit, suggesting a commander deck built around those cards. [Source]

Maular, the Next Evolution

Maular's Rulebreaker permission allows creature cards with mana value 7 or greater in the deck, including cards outside its color identity; the cycle also permits any basic land cards. [Source]

That suggests a commander deck focused on high-mana-value creatures and attacking, matching the article's description of its attack-related design. [Source]
