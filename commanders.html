MBC · Player guide

new mystery booster commanders

The eight new Mystery Booster Rulebreaker commanders are Seluma, Tolabow, The Unluckiest Planeswalker, Maular, Grizzlegom, Whtz, The Everforger, and Valko Indorian. Their shared feature is a specific deck-construction exception: most expand the color identity of selected card categories, while Whtz changes the maximum deck size. Wizards release notes

One mechanic, different boundaries

What these commanders have in common

These eight commanders use Rulebreaker to change a stated deck-construction restriction. The lineup has five monocolored commanders, two two-color commanders, and one colorless commander. Wizards release notes MTG Wiki: Mystery Booster Commander Edition

The color identity exceptions are category-specific: Angels for Seluma, a chosen extra spell color for Tolabow, Auras for The Unluckiest Planeswalker, large creatures for Maular, lands for Grizzlegom, artifact creatures and Equipment for The Everforger, and Phyrexians for Valko. Whtz is different: its exception removes the maximum deck size rather than opening extra colors. Wizards release notes

Rulebreaker applies when the card is your designated commander and changes only the restrictions its text mentions. It is not a blanket permission to use any card of any color. Wizards release notes

The Commander Format Panel participated in the Rulebreaker design. MTG Wiki: Mystery Booster Commander Edition

Our reading: the mechanic makes a commander's theme a deckbuilding boundary in its own right. The useful question is which exception advances the deck's plan, rather than how many colors can be added.
