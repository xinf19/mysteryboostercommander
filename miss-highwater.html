Menace

Whenever Miss Highwater deals combat damage to a player who doesn't have a contract counter, they may discard their hand. If they do, they draw seven cards and get a contract counter. For as long as they have a contract counter, when they lose the game, for each artifact and creature they controlled, create a token that's a copy of it.

Deck direction

Give Miss Highwater evasion or protection so you can offer a contract early. Pick opponents whose boards could be worth copying, but weigh the danger of giving them a fresh seven-card hand. You only collect if a player who took the deal later loses.

Combinations from Mystery Booster Commander

Olinda the Oblivious (MBC #17). Olinda’s odor counters put recurring pressure on opponents who accepted a contract. A player with a wide board may be an attractive deal target, but make sure the drain doesn’t push the wrong player out first.

Magus of the Chains. Magus replaces the seven-card refill after a player accepts the deal, while the contract counter can still be awarded. Explain this interaction before offering the deal.
