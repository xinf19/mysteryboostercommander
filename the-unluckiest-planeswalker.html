Rulebreaker — paraphrased

Aura cards may have any color identity, and the deck may include any basic land cards. [Card text]

Other abilities

Its +1 supplies mana and can add a Gremlin when that mana casts an Aura. Its −3 trades your hand for cards based on your Auras and moves Auras attached to you onto opponents. [Card text]

Deck direction

An Aura-and-Curse strategy built around its unusual enchantment permissions.

Why this design matters — our reading

My reading is that the narrow Aura permission preserves the character of a red commander while changing the tools around it. The interesting decision is which Auras support a single plan, not how many colors can fit in the list. I would make the Aura package and the loyalty abilities work toward the same outcome instead of treating every off-color Aura as automatically desirable.

Deckbuilding priorities — our reading

I would review the Aura package by role: cards that advance the plan, cards that keep it functioning, and cards that are only attractive because the permission makes them available. This separates an actual Aura strategy from a pile of exceptions. The commander's unusual permission is a reason to be selective, not a reason to skip that selection.
