Mystery Booster Commander Rules: Draft Partner and Playtest Card Legality

Mystery Booster Commander Edition adjusts Partner in Commander Draft and introduces Rulebreaker permissions that cross Commander color identity restrictions. It also introduces types and counters, while Playtest cards are limited to this set's Draft and Sealed events.

Card eligibility

Playtest cards are not legal in Constructed formats

The 121 Playtest cards can be used only in this set's Draft and Sealed events. They are not legal in any Constructed format.

This includes Constructed Commander.

What is not changing

  • Constructed deck size and singleton. The default is 100 cards including your commander or commanders, and one copy of each card other than basic lands. A card can override a specific limit, as Whtz, the Bibliophile does for maximum deck size.
  • Color identity. Cards in your Constructed deck must fit within your commander’s color identity, or the combined identity of both commanders. A Rulebreaker ability permits only the exceptions spelled out on that commander.
  • Starting life, commander tax, and commander damage. In multiplayer Commander, players still start at 40 life. Each previous cast of a commander from the command zone adds two mana to its next cast from there. Taking 21 combat damage from the same commander still causes a player to lose.
  • Partner in Constructed. The special Partner allowance applies to this set’s Commander Draft. Constructed decks still follow the usual rules for choosing two commanders.

How to play Mystery Booster Commander

For Mystery Booster Commander Draft, choose your commander with this set’s special Partner allowance in mind. A Rulebreaker ability changes deckbuilding only when its card is your commander. The 121 Playtest cards are limited to this set’s Draft and Sealed events.

  1. Before starting, identify whether you are playing this set’s Commander Draft or a Constructed Commander game.
  2. In this set’s Commander Draft, an eligible commander without Partner and with one or fewer colors in its color identity may be treated as having Partner.
  3. Choose your commander before applying its Rulebreaker ability during deckbuilding.
  4. Apply only the deckbuilding exception written on that commander.
  5. Use the 121 Playtest cards only in this set’s Draft and Sealed events.

Player questions

Frequently asked questions

Which commanders can be treated as having Partner in Commander Draft?

In Mystery Booster Commander Edition Commander Draft, an eligible commander without Partner whose color identity contains one or fewer colors may be treated as having Partner.

Can Mystery Booster Commander Playtest cards be used in Constructed Commander?

The 121 Playtest cards can be used only in this set's Draft and Sealed events. They are not legal in any Constructed format.

What does Rulebreaker do?

Rulebreaker gives a card a specific deckbuilding exception when you choose it as your commander. The ability applies before the game and changes only the restrictions described on the card.

Can I use Playtest cards in Commander?

No. Mystery Booster Commander Edition Playtest cards are limited to Mystery Booster Draft and Sealed under the official rules. A casual group can agree to allow them as a house rule.

Does MBC change normal Commander rules?

No. Constructed Commander still follows its usual deck size, singleton, and color identity rules unless a card explicitly provides an exception. The special Partner allowance applies to this set’s Commander Draft.
