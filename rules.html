mystery booster commander rules

Mystery Booster Commander Edition adjusts Partner in Commander Draft and introduces Rulebreaker permissions that cross Commander color identity restrictions. Wizards release notes It also introduces types and counters, while Playtest cards are limited to this set's Draft and Sealed events. MTG Wiki: Mystery Booster Commander Edition

Partner in Commander Draft and Rulebreaker

The Partner adjustment

In Commander Draft, a commander without Partner whose color identity contains one or fewer colors may be treated as having Partner. Wizards release notes
