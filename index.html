mystery booster commander game changes

Mystery Booster Commander Edition changes deckbuilding through Rulebreaker and adjusts Partner in Commander Draft, affecting Commander deckbuilders and Draft players; its listed Commander legality date is 2026-11-13. [Source] [Release notes]

Player questions

Frequently asked questions

Are the new Mystery Booster commanders legal?

The listed Commander legality date is 2026-11-13. [Source] The 121 Playtest cards can be used only in this set's Draft and Sealed events. They are not legal in any Constructed format. [Release notes]

How will the Mystery Booster commander work?

Rulebreaker allows cards outside a commander's color identity, directly changing deck construction rules. [Release notes] In Commander Draft, a commander without Partner whose color identity contains one or fewer colors may be treated as having Partner. [Release notes]

What is a Rulebreaker card?

Rulebreaker is a new mechanic appearing on 8 cards. It allows cards outside a commander's color identity. [Source] [Release notes]

When is Mystery Booster Commander Edition legal in Commander?

The listed Commander legality date is 2026-11-13. [Source] The 121 Playtest cards can be used only in this set's Draft and Sealed events. They are not legal in any Constructed format. [Release notes]
