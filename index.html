mystery booster commander game changes

Mystery Booster Commander Edition changes deckbuilding through Rulebreaker and adjusts Partner in Commander Draft, affecting Commander deckbuilders and Draft players; its listed Commander legality date is 2026-11-13. MTG Wiki: Mystery Booster Commander Edition Wizards release notes

Is it worth buying?

Access before value
