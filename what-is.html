what is mystery booster commander

Mystery Booster Commander Edition is the third Mystery Booster release, made for Commander Draft players with 20-card packs, Rulebreaker deckbuilding permissions, and Playtest cards restricted to this set's Draft and Sealed events. [Source] [Release notes]

Player questions

Frequently asked questions

What is Mystery Booster Commander Edition?

The third release in the Mystery Booster series. Its set code is MBC, and its 20-card packs are used for Commander Draft. [Source]

Can I use all Mystery Booster Commander cards in my Commander deck?

No. The 121 Playtest cards can be used only in this set's Draft and Sealed events. They are not legal in any Constructed format. [Release notes]
