The exception

How Rulebreaker changes color identity restrictions

Rulebreaker creates specific deck-construction exceptions, including permission for certain cards outside a commander's color identity and Whtz's deck-size exception. [Release notes]

The significance is the change to which cards can enter a deck. This may create different deckbuilding directions, but evaluating a particular commander requires its exact card text.

Disclosed names

The disclosed Rulebreaker commanders

The eight Rulebreaker commanders include five monocolored, two two-color, and one colorless commander. Explore each card's specific exception below. [Card text]

#4 · White

Seluma, Light of Aysen

{4}{W} · 4/4 [Card text]

Angel cards may have any color identity, and the deck may include any basic land cards. [Card text]

Flying; combat damage to a player lets Seluma return a target Angel creature card from your graveyard to the battlefield. [Card text]

Deck direction: Angel-focused combat with graveyard recovery.

#12 · Blue

Tolabow, Loch Rascal

{2}{U}{U} · 3/2 [Card text]

Choose one color outside the commander's color identity. Instant and sorcery cards may include that chosen color alongside blue, and the deck may include any basic land cards. This does not open the other card types to that color. [Card text]

Each instant or sorcery you cast makes a 1/1 blue and red Otter creature token with prowess. [Card text]

Deck direction: Instants and sorceries that build an Otter board, with one carefully chosen extra spell color.

#26 · Red

The Unluckiest Planeswalker

{2}{R} · Starting loyalty 4 [Card text]

Aura cards may have any color identity, and the deck may include any basic land cards. [Card text]

Its +1 supplies mana and can add a Gremlin when that mana casts an Aura. Its −3 trades your hand for cards based on your Auras and moves Auras attached to you onto opponents. [Card text]

Deck direction: An Aura-and-Curse strategy built around its unusual enchantment permissions.

#31 · Green

Maular, the Next Evolution

{5}{G}{G} · 10/10 [Card text]

Creature cards with mana value 7 or greater may have any color identity, and the deck may include any basic land cards. [Card text]

When one of your creatures with mana value 7 or greater attacks, its power and toughness double until the turn ends. [Card text]

Deck direction: High-mana-value creatures, a supporting mana plan, and attacking.

#39 · Red and green

Grizzlegom, Hurloon Hero

{5}{R}{G} · 4/4 [Card text]

The deck may include any land cards; the exception is for lands, not for every card type. [Card text]

Its attack ability counts the basic land types you control for five different payoffs. [Card text]

Deck direction: A red-green lands strategy that develops basic land types and attacks with its commander.

#57 · White and blue

Whtz, the Bibliophile

{1}{W}{U} · 1/3 [Card text]

The deck has no maximum deck size. This exception changes deck size, not color identity. [Card text]

{3}, {T}: draw a card and gain 1 life. If your starting deck contained 200 or more cards, the activation costs {3} less; the tap cost remains. [Card text]

Deck direction: A deliberately oversized white-blue deck built to benefit from the 200-card starting-deck threshold.

#59 · Colorless

The Everforger

{6} · 4/5 [Card text]

Artifact creature and Equipment cards may have any color identity, and the deck may include any basic land cards. [Card text]

Once each turn, you may copy an artifact creature or Equipment spell you cast; the copy becomes a token. [Card text]

Deck direction: Artifact creatures and Equipment, with deliberate choices about which eligible spell to copy each turn.

Read the commander overview →

#60 · Black

Valko Indorian

{3}{B} · 2/4 [Card text]

Phyrexian cards may have any color identity, and the deck may include any basic land cards. The permission refers to the Phyrexian creature type, not Phyrexian mana symbols. [Card text]

Phyrexian creatures you control have menace and lifelink. [Card text]

Deck direction: A Phyrexian creature strategy that turns its expanded selection into combat pressure.
