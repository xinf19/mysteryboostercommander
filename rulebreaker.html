The exception

How Rulebreaker changes color identity restrictions

Rulebreaker allows cards outside a commander's color identity, directly changing deck construction rules. [Release notes]

The significance is the change to which cards can enter a deck. This may create different deckbuilding directions, but evaluating a particular commander requires its exact card text.

Format design

The Commander Format Panel's involvement

The Commander Format Panel participated in the design of the Rulebreaker cards. [Source]

As a design interpretation, Rulebreaker makes deck construction itself a place for experimentation. Individual deck strength and wider format impact remain pending the card-specific details.
